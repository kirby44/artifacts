Privacy Policy: Claude Gmail Private

Last updated: 6 November 2026

"Claude Gmail Private" is a personal-use application run by Kazuki Kimura. It is used only by its owner to let a locally running assistant read, label and draft emails in the owner's own Gmail account.

  • The application is not offered to the public and has no other users.
  • Gmail data accessed through the application stays on the owner's own computer and is used only to help the owner manage their own email.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
